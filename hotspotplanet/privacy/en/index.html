Privacy Policy — Hotspot Planet

Privacy Policy

Languages: 한국어 · English · 日本語 · 简体中文 · 繁體中文 · Русский

App Name: Hotspot Planet Developer (Data Controller): INNOAUS KOREA INC. Effective Date: July 6, 2026 Last Updated: August 12, 2026

1. Overview

INNOAUS KOREA INC. (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. Hotspot Planet (“the app”) helps you store Wi-Fi credentials safely on your device and connect quickly. We do not collect or transmit your Wi-Fi credentials (SSID, password, etc.) to any server.

The app handles two kinds of information:

  • Stored on your device only (not collected): the Wi-Fi credentials you enter and in-app tip (donation) records.
  • Actually collected (sent off the device): only the Firebase crash/analytics data described in Section 2.

2. Information the App Handles

2-1. Wi-Fi Credentials You Enter (On-Device Only — Not Collected)

Item Detail
Stored items SSID (network name), password, security type, WPA-Enterprise username/server name, memo, icon
Storage location Your device only — passwords and other secrets are kept in the iOS Keychain (or the app’s private secure storage on Android)
Server transmission None — never collected, sent to, or accessed by our servers

Note: Information shown by widgets (via App Group, etc.) does not include passwords or other secrets.

2-2. Location (Only to Read the Current Wi-Fi Name)

Operating systems require location permission to read the name (SSID) of the currently connected Wi-Fi network. We use location permission solely for this purpose. We do not collect, store, or transmit your real-time location (coordinates).

2-3. Camera (QR Code Scanning Only)

The camera is used only to scan Wi-Fi QR codes when adding a hotspot. Captured images are not stored or transmitted; only the Wi-Fi information read from the QR code is saved on your device.

2-4. Backup / Restore

If you choose to export a backup, the file is created in a location you specify on your device. ⚠️ Backup files may contain Wi-Fi passwords — please be careful when sharing them. You are responsible for managing your backup files.

2-5. Crash Reports & Analytics (Firebase)

To improve stability and usability, we use Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics (Google LLC). The following may be sent to Firebase:

Collected Item Detail
Crash stack trace Error location and cause
Device model / OS version Device type and OS version
App version / build number Version info needed to reproduce errors
Usage events Usage statistics such as screen transitions
App-instance identifier A pseudonymous identifier used by Analytics (e.g., IDFV) — not linked to your name or email

This data is not used to identify you by name. Google’s privacy policy applies: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

2-6. In-App Purchases (Developer Support)

The app offers optional, consumable in-app purchases (voluntary tips). They do not unlock features or remove ads.

  • Payment processing: All payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never collect, store, or access your payment details (card numbers, etc.).
  • On-device only (not collected): Your support history (transaction identifiers, total amount, currency, support points) and an optional badge nickname (up to 2 characters) are stored only on your device.

2-7. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Wi-Fi credentials you enter (SSID, password, security type, username, server name, memo) — on-device only
  • Name, email address, phone number, or other identifying information
  • Real-time device location (coordinates)
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA / AAID)
  • Payment details for in-app purchases (handled by Apple/Google)

3. Purposes of Use

  • Connecting quickly to your saved Wi-Fi networks
  • Displaying the name of the currently connected network
  • Improving app stability and diagnosing errors (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Analyzing de-identified usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)
  • Processing developer-support purchases and displaying the supporter badge

4. Retention & Deletion

  • Wi-Fi credentials are stored only on your device.
  • Deleting the app removes all app data on the device (saved list and secrets).
  • You can delete any backup files you created at any time.
  • Firebase crash/analytics data is retained according to Google’s retention settings and then deleted or aggregated.

5. Third Parties & International Transfers

We do not sell your personal information. The following third-party services are used and are governed by their own privacy policies:

Service Provider Purpose Privacy Policy
Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics Google LLC Crash reporting & analytics firebase.google.com/support/privacy
App Store In-App Purchase Apple Inc. Tip payment processing apple.com/legal/privacy
Google Play Billing Google LLC Tip payment processing policies.google.com/privacy

Firebase data is processed on Google’s servers, which may be located outside your country (including the United States). Google participates in recognized transfer frameworks and applies safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) where required.

Controller: INNOAUS KOREA INC. (contact: support@innoaus.com)

Legal bases for processing:

Usage analytics (Analytics) Consent where required by local law (Art. 6(1)(a)), otherwise legitimate interests
Tip purchase processing Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b))

Your rights: You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior processing). You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, contact support@innoaus.com. Because we hold almost no personal data (most data stays on your device), the simplest way to erase everything is to delete the app.

7. For California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

In the last 12 months, the only categories of personal information we have collected are device identifiers and internet/app activity (the Firebase data in Section 2-5), used solely for app stability and analytics.

  • We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information.
  • You have the right to know, correct, and delete personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights.

To exercise your rights, contact support@innoaus.com.

8. Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction, e.g., 14 in Korea, 16 in parts of the EEA). If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Choices

  • Delete saved Wi-Fi items in the app at any time
  • Revoke location/camera permissions in system settings
  • Remove all local data by deleting the app
